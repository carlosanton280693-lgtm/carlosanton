I care about the systems behind the people work: whether an onboarding request actually clears every approval, whether a dashboard tells a leader the truth about SLA risk, whether an audit finding turns into a fixed process instead of a repeated one.

Over 8 years at Estée Lauder Companies (OneSource) I've worked both sides of that — validating and auditing the data as a QA Analyst, then owning it as a supervisor managing up to 16 HR Service Representatives and 1 Team Lead across US, LATAM, Canada, and I-9 populations.

Bilingual in Spanish and English, comfortable in fast-paced remote environments that need accuracy, discretion, and someone who follows through.

How I work

Good HR operations should be invisible — until someone needs them to be perfect.

I'd rather build the control, form, or automation that prevents the error than write the report explaining it afterward. Compliance and employee experience aren't in tension — a clean process is what makes both possible.
